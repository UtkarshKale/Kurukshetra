## 2026-10-13 (late night) - day/night, fog, night vision, difficulty, recommendation
- Time of day (Day / Dusk / Night) and visibility (Clear / Hazy / Foggy) are fields on the run config, buttons on the trainer panel, and can be set by Randomize (chances are fields on TrainerScreen: night 20%, dusk 20%, hazy 25%, foggy 10%).
- TimeOfDayController (new Environment object in the scene) applies the look at run start, at runtime only: sun height, colour and intensity, flat ambient, reflection strength, a dimmed runtime copy of the sky box, and exponential-squared fog. Day + Clear is the user's scene untouched; the original light, ambient, sky and fog values are captured at start and restored whenever Day is chosen and when the component is disabled. The user's sky box asset is never edited.
- Gameplay effect, all in Data/EnvironmentProfile_Default.asset: minimum visible apparent size x1.4 at dusk and x2 at night; max visible range 60% when hazy and 35% when foggy; fog density is tied to that range.
- Night vision: N toggles it at dusk and night. Green wash, moving grain and a dark surround drawn in code (no post-processing or URP asset changes), plus brighter green scene lighting so the picture really is brighter. It recovers 60% of the time-of-day penalty (night x2 becomes x1.4). HUD shows "NV ON (N)" or "N: night vision". Never shows contact class.
- Session result and JSON now record time of day, visibility, night-vision share of the run and difficulty; the dashboard shows "Difficulty: ... Conditions: Night, Hazy, NV used 80%".
- Difficulty levels in Data/Difficulty/ (builder's defaults): Recruit (contacts x0.7, decoys x0.7 more, speed x0.8, size threshold x0.8, AI wrong 8% / 20%), Trained (all x1, AI 15% / 35%, same as before), Expert (contacts x1.4, decoys x1.5 more, speed x1.25, one haze level worse, size threshold x1.25, AI 25% / 45%). Applied on top of the preset or randomized scenario at START; the working values on the panel stay unscaled so levels never stack. Randomize takes its degraded-sensor and ROE-flip chances from the selected level.
- Recommendation: score % = total points / (8 x scored contacts), clamped to 0-100. Strictly above 80% recommends one level harder, strictly below 50% one easier, otherwise the same. Shown on the trainer panel after a run and in the dashboard, each with an Apply button; never applied automatically. Thresholds are fields on TrainerScreen.
- Verified through the CLI: Dusk/Hazy, Night/Clear, Night/Foggy and Day/Foggy each set the expected light, ambient, sky copy, fog density and sensor multipliers (e.g. Night/Foggy: sun 0.06, fog 0.00286, size x2.0, range 700 m); returning to Day/Clear restored sun 1.0 at 50 degrees, sky-box ambient, the original sky box and fog off; after leaving Play mode the scene was unchanged and not dirty. Recruit gave 2/1/1/3 contacts at speed 0.8, Expert 4/4/4/13 at speed 1.25 with Hazy. A 30 s night run with night vision on for 80% of it scored 10/32 = 31% and recommended Recruit. Camera captures show night nearly black and night vision green and brighter. Self-tests all passing: environment 18, trainer 24, scoring 34, dashboard 14, sensors 21. Console clean.

## 2026-10-13 (night) - reversible Hold, score dashboard
- Hold is no longer final. A held contact can be re-called (1-5) and acted on with R, J or F; Hold again does nothing. Report and Jam stay final; Engage still repeats after a miss. The contact is scored by its final action and the ROE card in force then; still holding at the end scores as Hold. Lock label shows "HELD - R / J / F to act".
- Hold-delay rule (builder's defaults, editable in Data/ScoringRules_Default.asset): a hostile held while the card is UNDER ATTACK loses 1 point per full 15 s before the final action, capped at -3. No penalty under Weapons Hold or on non-hostiles. Hold start, hold time, time held under attack and call-to-action latency are recorded per contact.
- Score dashboard replaces the F5 overlay. F5 no longer does anything; the SCORE button on the trainer panel (F6) is the only way in. It opens in the trainer's half with Expand / Collapse / Close. Sections: player roster (Player 1, live state and score), big numbers (score, avg detection, classification accuracy, engagement decisions), counts, confusion heatmap, replay timeline per contact, decision tree per contact as three lit boxes with AI hint / hold time / shots, trend charts over the last 10 saved sessions (padded with dashed, labelled sample points when fewer exist), and a "UNIT (placeholder, sample data)" table of 6 sample soldiers.
- Data sources are behind IPlayerSource and IUnitStatsSource (Scripts/Trainer/DashboardData.cs) so multiplayer and unit data can replace the placeholders without touching the drawing code.
- Fixed on the way: the lock label had stopped showing REPORTED / JAMMED / ENGAGED after the engage-after-miss change.
