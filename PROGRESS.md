## 2026-10-20 - Polish pass 1-3: invisible birds, stray chart lines, real charts
- Birds, root cause: nothing was wrong with the material, matrices, culling or the pose meshes (a bird at 12 m renders fine). A bird is drawn at its true size (kite 1.8 m, pigeon 0.8 m, sparrow 0.3 m) and the wings are flat strips seen almost edge on from the ground, so beyond about 150 m the triangles are under a pixel thick and nothing is rasterised (a bird at 300 to 900 m produced 0 to 2 faint pixels, drones got away with it because their bodies are solid). Fix: `BirdView` (Birds/BirdMesh.cs): a bird narrower than 8 px on screen is drawn as the flat far silhouette turned to face the camera, never narrower than 7 px (made bigger, not smaller; detection and scoring still use the true size). Applies to single birds (BirdBody), small flocks, and big flocks (BirdDrawJob `minSpanPx`). Not applied to crowd-pattern birds (CrowdJobs) or the replay.
- Charts, stray lines: `DashboardCharts.Line` (used by the SCORE > TRENDS charts) rotated a strip with `RotateAroundPivot` under the dashboard's scaled `GUI.matrix`, which puts the pivot in the wrong place (same family as the replay green lines). It now delegates to `GuiDraw.Line` (clipped; squares under a scaled matrix). The old `TrendChart` and the card `Histogram` / `LineChart` (which used `LassoMath.Line`) were removed.
- Charts, real visualisations: Scoring/ChartModels.cs (six chart models from a SessionResult and the saved sessions: score by session with pass mark and 3-session average, detection time by session with a target and gaps, classification and engagement, what went wrong, calls by true class as stacked bars split by what was called, detection-time histogram with median and target; empty-state text; sample points are skipped, nothing is invented), ChartLayout.cs (one layout: titles, axis titles with units, ticks, legend, value labels, reference lines, hover areas with tooltip text), Trainer/ChartKit.cs (IMGUI, each chart in its own clipped group, hover tooltips), Scoring/ChartSvg.cs (the same layout as inline SVG with `<title>` tooltips). SCORE > Charts tab is now these six in a scroll view (the XCharts texture panel is no longer used); the TRENDS section and the Detection / Trend cards use them; the HTML report has a Cards / Charts switch (radio buttons + CSS, no script, offline).

## 2026-10-13 (late night) - day/night, fog, night vision, difficulty, recommendation
- Time of day (Day / Dusk / Night) and visibility (Clear / Hazy / Foggy) are fields on the run config, buttons on the trainer panel, and can be set by Randomize (chances are fields on TrainerScreen: night 20%, dusk 20%, hazy 25%, foggy 10%).
- TimeOfDayController (new Environment object in the scene) applies the look at run start, at runtime only: sun height, colour and intensity, flat ambient, reflection strength, a dimmed runtime copy of the sky box, and exponential-squared fog. Day + Clear is the user's scene untouched; the original light, ambient, sky and fog values are captured at start and restored whenever Day is chosen and when the component is disabled. The user's sky box asset is never edited.
- Gameplay effect, all in Data/EnvironmentProfile_Default.asset: minimum visible apparent size x1.4 at dusk and x2 at night; max visible range 60% when hazy and 35% when foggy; fog density is tied to that range.
- Night vision: N toggles it at dusk and night. Green wash, moving grain and a dark surround drawn in code (no post-processing or URP asset changes), plus brighter green scene lighting so the picture really is brighter. It recovers 60% of the time-of-day penalty (night x2 becomes x1.4). HUD shows "NV ON (N)" or "N: night vision". Never shows contact class.
- Session result and JSON now record time of day, visibility, night-vision share of the run and difficulty; the dashboard shows "Difficulty: ... Conditions: Night, Hazy, NV used 80%".

Applied on top of the preset or randomized scenario at START; the working values on the panel stay unscaled so levels never stack. Randomize takes its degraded-sensor and ROE-flip chances from the selected level.
